Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account, along with the expense records you log — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, to keep the Service running reliably.

How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate the Service: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating the summaries and breakdowns you see in the app. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage

Your account details and expense records are stored in our database for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, after which your data will be permanently removed within a reasonable period.

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party providers for infrastructure such as hosting and email delivery. These providers only process data as needed to support the Service and are not permitted to use your information for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
